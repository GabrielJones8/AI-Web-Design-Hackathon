The product / 002

A basketball designed from the inside out.

Orbit Ball uses 3D printing to rethink how a basketball can be tuned, tested, and made for a consistent response.

How it works

Control the variables that players feel.

Instead of treating the ball as a fixed shell around an inflated bladder, our approach uses additive manufacturing to shape its structure layer by layer. That gives us a clearer path to test density, texture, and geometry as a connected system.

The goal is not to make the game robotic. It is to reduce the distracting variation between sessions so players can trust their touch, their pass, and their shot.

What it can provide

Built for the details between the highlights.

01 / RESPONSE

A familiar bounce

A carefully considered internal structure is intended to keep the ball's response more predictable as conditions shift.

02 / CONTROL

Reliable handling

Surface and geometry work together to support a consistent grip for dribbling, passing, and shooting repetitions.

03 / FEEDBACK

Designed to be tested

3D printing makes it possible to prototype quickly, compare variables, and keep improving from real player feedback.

04 / PURPOSE

Practice without translation

Train with a ball that aims to feel close to game day, so your work carries over with less adjustment.
