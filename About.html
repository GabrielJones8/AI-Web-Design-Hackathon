Our story / 001

It started with a ball that felt different.

Orbit Ball began with a simple question: why should the game change just because the air does?

The beginning

Notice the problem. Make it tangible.

Players told us the same story: in the mountains, on colder mornings, or after a long session, their ball did not always respond the way they expected. The difference was small, but small differences add up when you are building a shot.

So we started testing. We explored digital fabrication, flexible structures, and the relationship between a ball's surface and its core. Orbit Ball is the result of that ongoing work: a 3D-printed basketball concept built around a more controlled, repeatable feel.

What we believe

Better tools leave more room for instinct.

“When the ball feels familiar, you can spend your attention on the next play.”
